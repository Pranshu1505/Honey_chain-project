🎯 Key Features

📍 Beekeeper Management

Track beekeeper profiles, experience, certifications, and honey yields across your apiaries.

🐝 Hive Monitoring

Real-time monitoring of hive health, population, and conditions with IoT sensors.

🍯 Batch Tracking

Complete supply chain tracking from harvest to distribution with status updates.

✅ Quality Testing

Comprehensive quality tests including acidity, moisture, color, and aroma grading.

⛓️ Blockchain Verification

Immutable records of honey batches on blockchain with SHA256 verification.

📱 QR Code Integration

Generate and verify QR codes for batch tracking and consumer verification.
