❓ What is Honey Chain?

Honey Chain is a blockchain-based platform for tracking honey from production to consumer. It provides complete traceability, quality assurance, and authenticity verification for honey products.

❓ How does blockchain verification work?

Each honey batch is assigned a unique hash using SHA256 cryptography. This hash is recorded on the blockchain, creating an immutable record. Consumers can verify the batch by scanning a QR code, which checks the hash against the blockchain.

❓ Who can use Honey Chain?

Honey Chain is designed for beekeepers, producers, quality inspectors, distributors, retailers, and consumers. Each user type has specific features and permissions tailored to their role.

❓ How do I register my hive?

Log in to your dashboard and navigate to "Register Hive". Provide details about your hive including location, beekeeper information, and any IoT sensors. Once registered, your hive data will be monitored in real-time.

❓ What quality tests are performed?

Our quality testing includes: acidity level measurement, moisture content analysis, color grading, aroma assessment, and purity verification. All results are recorded and available on the batch record.

❓ How can consumers verify honey authenticity?

Each honey batch comes with a unique QR code. Consumers can scan this code with their smartphone to view the complete batch history, quality tests, and blockchain verification status.

❓ What IoT sensors do you support?

We support various temperature, humidity, and population sensors. Our system integrates with popular IoT platforms to collect real-time data about hive conditions.

❓ How is my data protected?

We use token-based JWT authentication, encrypted connections (HTTPS), role-based access control, and regular security audits. Your data is hosted securely and backed up regularly.

❓ What are the pricing plans?

Contact our sales team for custom pricing. We offer plans for individual beekeepers, small producers, and enterprise clients. Each plan includes different features and support levels.

❓ How do I get technical support?

Email us at support@honeychain.com or fill out our contact form. We typically respond within 24 hours during business days.

❓ Can I integrate with my existing systems?

Yes, our REST API allows integration with existing systems. We also provide webhooks for real-time event notifications. Contact our development team for integration support.

❓ What happens if I lose my account credentials?

You can reset your password using the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. You'll receive an email with instructions to create a new password.
